FOps Studio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Who we are

FOps Studio is a private, self-hosted application operated by FutureOps (contact: futureopsltd@gmail.com). It is used only by the owner of the FutureOps YouTube channels to manage those channels. It is not offered to the public.

Google user data we access

When the channel owner connects a channel, FOps Studio requests access through Google OAuth to:

  • YouTube channel and video metadata (YouTube Data API, read-only);
  • YouTube Analytics reports, including views, watch time and estimated revenue for the owner's channels (YouTube Analytics API);
  • uploading videos and setting their metadata and scheduled publish time on the owner's own channels (YouTube Data API, upload).

FOps Studio does not access Gmail, Drive, contacts or any Google data outside the scopes listed above.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show channel performance to the owner, to plan new videos, and to upload and schedule the owner's own videos. It is not used for advertising, not used to train general-purpose AI models, and not sold, rented or shared with any third party.

Where the data is stored

Data is stored on the owner's own computers on a private home network. OAuth refresh credentials are stored encrypted and are excluded from regular backups. Analytics snapshots are kept for a limited time and refreshed from YouTube.

Limited Use

FOps Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or by disconnecting the channel in FOps Studio. On disconnection the stored credentials and private analytics snapshots for that channel are deleted. Deletion requests can be sent to futureopsltd@gmail.com.

YouTube

FOps Studio uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; the Google Privacy Policy applies to data held by Google.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date.
